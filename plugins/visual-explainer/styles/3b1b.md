# 3Blue1Brown

The look of 3Blue1Brown videos: manim's palette on black, Computer Modern serif, square corners. Fits math, physics, algorithms, signals and geometry: a quantity drawn, then changed step by step. Dark only.

## Color

- One color names one concept for the whole page: figures, equations and text. Pick the mapping first.
- Yellow (`--yellow`, the kit accent) is the thing to look at now. One yellow thing at a time.
- One blue, one or two warm colors, and greys. Four hues at most.
- Convention: x green, y red, z `--blue-d`. An equation term takes the color of the object it names.
- `c-<color>` on an SVG part or a text `<span>` sets its color: `blue teal green yellow gold red maroon purple pink orange grey`.

## Figures

- On the page background: no card, border, shadow, glow or gradient.
- Draw only what the idea needs. Add the next object when the text asks for it.
- Math figures use `viewBox="0 0 1280 720"` and these SVG parts:
  - `axis`, `tick`: grey lines. `grid`, `grid-faint`: the plane, filling the figure.
  - `curve`: an open stroked path. `shape`: a closed path at 25% fill. Both take a `c-*` color.
  - `vec`: an arrow drawn as one filled polygon. From (0,0) to (L,0): `0,-2 L-17.5,-2 L-17.5,-10 L,0 L-17.5,10 L-17.5,2 0,2`, then rotate.
  - `dot`: a filled circle, r 7.2. `label`: text in its object's color with a black halo, 22 units from it; `small` for 24px.
  - `thin`, `dashed`: modifiers.
- Diagrams of systems use the kit's nodes and edges: thin grey outlines, the key node and hot path in blue, the current step in yellow.
- Equations: KaTeX from jsDelivr, colored terms with `\textcolor{#FFFF00}{s}`. Build an equation up step by step with the changed terms colored.

## Motion

- Easing is manim's `smooth` (the kit's `--ease`). JS: `t => t*t*t*(10 - 15*t + 6*t*t)`.
- One change 1 s, a figure appearing or an equation transforming 2 s.
- Reveal by drawing a stroke in, fading in, morphing one shape or equation into the next, or turning a term yellow and back.
- Axes, grid, captions and text never move. Nothing loops or plays without a reader's action.

## Layout

- Concrete example first, then the picture, then the general statement, then the formula or name.
- Text column 43.75rem; figures up to 62.5rem. Titles, section headings and captions are centered.
