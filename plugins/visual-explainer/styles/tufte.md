# Tufte

The look of Tufte CSS: cream page, ET Book serif, italic section heads, a narrow text column with captions in the right margin. Fits a text-led page with small, dense, static figures beside the text that discusses them. Light and dark.

- Ink goes to data. Thin, short axes (`axis`), faint gridlines only where values must be read (`grid`).
- Greyscale first. One `accent` element per figure marks what the text is about.
- Labels sit directly on the lines and points they name, in the text face. No legend box.
- Change over time is small multiples or a `<svg class="sparkline">` inside a sentence.
- No motion. A figure may reveal a value on hover, without a transition.
- No 3D, shadows, gradients, chart borders, plot backgrounds or pictograms.
