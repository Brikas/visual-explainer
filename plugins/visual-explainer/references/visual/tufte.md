# Tufte

The look of Tufte CSS: a cream page, the ET Book serif, italic section headings and a narrow text column with captions in the right margin. It fits a text-led page with small, dense, static figures beside the text that discusses them. Light and dark.

- Ink goes to data. Axes (`axis`) are thin and short. Gridlines (`grid`) appear only where the reader must read values.
- Figures are greyscale. One `accent` element per figure marks what the text is about.
- Labels sit directly on the lines and points they name, in the text face. Figures have no legend box.
- Change over time is shown as small multiples or as `<svg class="sparkline">` inside a sentence.
- Figures do not move. A figure may show a value on hover with no transition.
- Figures have no 3D, shadows, gradients, chart borders, plot backgrounds or pictograms.
