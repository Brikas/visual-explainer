# 3Blue1Brown

The look of 3Blue1Brown videos: manim's palette on black, the Computer Modern serif and square corners. It fits math, physics, algorithms, signals and geometry. In these subjects a quantity is drawn and then changed step by step. Dark only.

## Color

- One color names one concept for the whole page. The concept keeps the color in figures, equations and text. Pick the mapping first.
- Yellow (`--yellow`, the kit accent) marks the thing to look at now. One yellow thing at a time.
- A page uses one blue, one or two warm colors and greys. Four hues at most.
- Axis colors: x is green, y is red, z is `--blue-d`.
- An equation term takes the color of the object it names.
- `c-<color>` on an SVG part or a text `<span>` sets its color. Colors: `blue teal green yellow gold red maroon purple pink orange grey`.

## Figures

- Figures sit on the page background. They have no card, border, shadow, glow or gradient.
- Draw only what the idea needs. Add the next object when the text asks for it.
- Math figures use `viewBox="0 0 1280 720"` and these SVG parts:
  - `axis` and `tick`: grey lines.
  - `grid` and `grid-faint`: the plane. The grid fills the figure.
  - `curve`: an open stroked path. It takes a `c-*` color.
  - `shape`: a closed path at 25% fill. It takes a `c-*` color.
  - `vec`: an arrow drawn as one filled polygon. From (0,0) to (L,0) the points are `0,-2 L-17.5,-2 L-17.5,-10 L,0 L-17.5,10 L-17.5,2 0,2`. Rotate the polygon to aim it.
  - `dot`: a filled circle with radius 7.2.
  - `label`: text in its object's color with a black halo. It sits 22 units from the object. `small` sets it to 24px.
  - `thin` and `dashed`: stroke modifiers.
- System diagrams use the kit's nodes and edges. Outlines are thin and grey. The key node and the hot path are blue. The current step is yellow.
- Equations use KaTeX from jsDelivr. Color a term with `\textcolor{#FFFF00}{s}`. Build an equation up step by step. The changed terms carry color.

## Motion

- The easing is manim's `smooth` function (the kit's `--ease`). In JS: `t => t*t*t*(10 - 15*t + 6*t*t)`.
- One change takes 1 s. A figure appearing or an equation transforming takes 2 s.
- Reveals: a stroke draws in, a part fades in, one shape or equation morphs into the next, or a term turns yellow and back.
- Axes, grid, captions and text stay still. Motion starts only on the reader's action. Nothing loops.

## Layout

- Order: a concrete example, the picture, the general statement, the formula or name.
- The text column is 43.75rem wide. Figures are up to 62.5rem wide.
- Titles, section headings and captions are centered. Body text is flush left.
